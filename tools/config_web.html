Wi-Fi Wake-on-LAN

When the controller connects (PS press) and the PC has not enumerated the dongle, the dongle immediately joins this 2.4 GHz network and broadcasts magic packets for the PC's network card until the PC comes up (5 min max). Wi-Fi is off at all other times. Saved together with the settings above.
